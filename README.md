# Simple Interest Calculator

## Introduction

A **Simple Interest Calculator** is a tool that computes the interest earned or owed on a principal amount over a period of time at a given rate of interest, using the simple interest formula.

---

## What is Simple Interest?

Simple Interest is calculated only on the **principal amount** (the original sum of money), unlike compound interest which is calculated on both the principal and the accumulated interest.

---

## Formula

$$SI = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$

Where:

| Variable | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **SI** | Simple Interest |
| **P** | Principal Amount (initial amount of money) |
| **R** | Rate of Interest (per annum, in %) |
| **T** | Time Period (in years) |

**Total Amount:**

$$A = P + SI$$

---

## Example

> **Problem:** Calculate the simple interest on a principal of ₹5,000 at a rate of 8% per annum for 3 years.

**Solution:**

$$SI = \frac{5000 \times 8 \times 3}{100} = \frac{120000}{100} = ₹1200$$

$$A = 5000 + 1200 = ₹6200$$

---

## Usage

This calculator takes three inputs:

1. **Principal (P)** – The initial amount of money (e.g., loan amount or investment)
2. **Rate of Interest (R)** – The percentage of interest per year
3. **Time (T)** – The duration in years

And outputs:

- **Simple Interest (SI)**
- **Total Amount (A)**

---

## Applications

- Bank loans
- Fixed deposits
- Car or home loans (short-term)
- Savings accounts
